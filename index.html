Becca Scott is an actor, internet nerd, and performer best known for turning tabletop games into hours of watchable, rewatchable chaos. She's spent years in front of the camera as a host and storyteller, and behind it as a founder and producer.

She's the co-founder and CEO of Good Time Society, the YouTube channel where a lot of that chaos lives — board game tutorials, actual-play series, and the kind of content made by people who genuinely love games rather than just talking about them.

Outside of Good Time Society, she shows up across the geek-media landscape — from scripted voice work to live tabletop shows to hosting gigs — and somehow also finds time to be, in her own words, a "badass fuckin' mother."
